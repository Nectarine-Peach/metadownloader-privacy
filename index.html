Nectarine Peach provides Meta Downloader (com.metadownloader). The app stores information on your device to maintain login and save media. The developer does not operate a server that receives your login sessions or downloaded files.

1. Information stored on your device

  • Login: account IDs, available usernames and session cookies for Instagram and Threads. App session copies are encrypted using the device key store; WebView cookies and storage are managed separately. The app has no password-entry form and does not store your password.
  • Downloads: selected media, source and media URLs, file names, storage locations, task states and history. Separate duplicate-prevention records retain the service, media type, source identifier and media URL path.
  • Preferences: destination-folder access, naming and history settings, and other app preferences.
  • Optional age range: selected in Settings and stored locally. The app is intended for users aged 13 or older; selecting under 13 restricts use. No date of birth or identity document is collected. This self-declared range is not age verification or a substitute for legally required parental consent.

2. Connections to external services

Signing in, browsing, looking up a link or downloading sends necessary requests to Meta services and media servers. They receive your IP address and the request information needed for that feature, including service authentication cookies where required. Their own privacy policies apply, and processing may occur outside your country. The app does not create or delete these external accounts.

3. Advertising

The app may use Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google may process IP addresses (including approximate location derived from them), device-related information, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Login data, media addresses, file names and download history are not supplied as custom advertising parameters.

All eligible users receive the same restricted ad treatment, regardless of age selection: personalized advertising and remarketing are restricted and the maximum ad content rating is PG. Ad requests wait for UMP eligibility. Because under-consent-age treatment is enabled, UMP does not request advertising consent. Settings shows “Ad privacy options” when required and provided by the SDK. Restricted ads do not mean that all data processing stops or replace any separate consent required by law.

For Google's processing, retention, recipients and international operations, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. External providers determine their own retention under their policies.

4. Managing and deleting information

  • Logging out removes that service's saved app session and clears shared WebView cookies and storage. This can affect other services' WebView logins; their separate saved app sessions may remain.
  • Clearing history removes finished display records, not active tasks or saved files. Duplicate-prevention records remain separately; they have no automatic expiry or dedicated in-app deletion control.
  • Android's app-data deletion or uninstall removes internal app data, including login and preferences. Files saved in shared storage or a chosen folder may remain; delete them with a file manager or gallery.

The app uses HTTPS and encrypted app-session storage, and restricts backup and device transfer of internal data. Notification permission and access to a chosen folder are requested through Android when used.

5. Support and privacy requests

Contact dev2peach@gmail.com. Your sender address and message are used to respond and handle requests, retained while needed for that purpose and then deleted, subject to legal retention duties. Do not send passwords, session cookies or unnecessary sensitive information.

You may request access, correction, deletion or other rights available under applicable law. Identity or authority may need to be checked for an email request. For information held only by an external provider, contact that provider. Parents or guardians may also contact us about a child's information.

6. Documents and updates

In-app documents are bundled and can be read offline. Opening the public website or an external reference sends request information to that provider. The public site uses GitHub Pages, has no developer-added ads, analytics or login forms, and is covered by GitHub's Privacy Statement. We update this policy and its effective date when practices change; the public copy may be newer than the copy bundled with your app.
